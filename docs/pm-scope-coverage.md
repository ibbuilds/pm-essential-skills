# PM capability coverage

The product scope is the supporting workflow across domains, not just the first
skills implemented. A completed change, passing check, or published package is a
milestone; completion is judged against the full requested outcome.

## Responsibilities and owners

| Capability | Primary owner | Supporting existing capability |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Task intake, scoping, proportionate planning, feasibility, priorities, change impact, dependencies, and completion | Task Coordination | Context Management handles actual conversation handoffs; Execution Efficiency handles verification and recovery. |
| Context and project knowledge | Context Management | Source Selection retains provenance, coverage, and freshness; Workflow Improvement reconciles material changes in existing authoritative knowledge. |
| Model, agent, tool, reasoning, and usage choices | Resource Selection | Source Selection owns retrieval decisions; Execution Efficiency owns check/retry decisions. |
| Choosing and adapting skills, instructions, templates, and deterministic tools | Workflow Improvement | Existing domain owners keep their specialized procedures; installation and validation support distribution. |
| Recurring-error prevention and continuous improvement | Workflow Improvement | Execution Efficiency applies known lessons during repair; Context Management carries applicable findings through handoffs. |
| Workflow evaluation and governance | Workflow Improvement | Task Coordination preserves scope and acceptance; the optional complete-task evaluator handles supplied usage data. |
| Brief, specific, plain-language communication | Concise Communication | Domain skills still deliver complete requested artifacts, evidence, and necessary warnings. |

These are capability boundaries, not seven mandatory steps or seven instructions
to load for every request. Each skill works independently. Use only the relevant
owner; retain already-loaded guidance rather than rereading companion skills.

## Acceptance conditions

- A clear small request proceeds directly without an intake interview, tracker,
  management file, or delegation ceremony.
- Material missing decisions get short, actionable questions. Known user choices
  are reused, not requested again.
- Larger outcomes retain their acceptance criteria, dependencies, exclusions,
  owners, and unresolved requested work through implementation and delivery.
- Material changes trigger a proportionate feasibility and commitment check.
  Conflicting scope, time, budget, capacity, or quality constraints get a concrete
  tradeoff decision rather than silent cuts, overruns, or obsolete work. Harmless
  changes do not restart planning.
- Resource choices use capabilities actually exposed by the host and authorized
  by the user. No model changes, external services, or permissions are invented.
- Useful knowledge is reused with its applicability and evidence intact. Changed
  facts or conflicting instructions are reconciled at their existing owner rather
  than copied into another rulebook.
- Repeated mechanical work can justify a deterministic helper; a distinct repeated
  workflow can justify a skill. A one-off inconvenience does not justify either.
- Quality requirements and requested coverage remain binding. Verification is
  proportionate to the risk and preserves required domain gates.
- Replies are short by default, not incomplete. Detailed requests and complete
  artifacts remain detailed when needed.
- Installation, individual selection, additive upgrades, optional reminders,
  recovery, and uninstall preserve unrelated user files and preferences.

## Boundaries

Product strategy, marketing, UI design methods, application architecture, and
deployment procedures belong to their own domain workflows. PM coordinates their
requested outcomes without prescribing those domains' methods. No particular
tracker, integration, model provider, or background service is required.

Coverage means the responsibility has an implemented owner and relevant cases;
it is not a claim that every host invokes skills identically. Deterministic checks
and structural fixtures establish their respective mechanics, not measured live
savings. Usage evaluation remains optional and must not block independently
justified improvements when telemetry or execution allowance is unavailable.

## General foundation coverage review

The October 22, 2026 static review traced research synthesis, campaigns, events,
reports/spreadsheets, design assets, writing, and ongoing operations. It identified
change-impact feasibility and prioritization as underdeveloped within Task
Coordination, not a reason for an eighth owner. The conditional rule and three
additional scenarios cover a slipped prerequisite, conflicting added scope, and a
harmless change. These scenarios are specifications, not executed model evidence.

Seven is the current organization, not a permanent limit or proof of universal
completeness. Add a standalone skill only for a distinct recurring responsibility
with a clear trigger that existing owners cannot cover more simply. Specialist
methods and host-enforced capabilities remain separate from this shared foundation.

## Cross-suite research application (2026-10-22)

Today's SWE research was screened against PM's existing owners, not copied as a
second engineering workflow. One missing coordination procedure warranted a
narrow original adaptation: check that a prerequisite's promised deliverable
meets the consuming step's needs before dependent work starts. Task Coordination
already named dependencies and required final integration, but neither explicitly
caught an incompatible handoff early. This applies to research breakdowns, design
assets, and other non-code work as well as software.

The source lead was Superpowers'
[writing-plans interfaces and consistency checks](https://github.com/obra/superpowers/blob/2ce8aa15d05bd57213e6f746f953aafb7ded7a8b/skills/writing-plans/SKILL.md).
PM adopts no mandatory plan, test sequence, reviewer, fixed number of findings,
or approval stage from that workflow. An already-compatible shared specification
and applicable domain validation are reused, not subjected to a second technical
gate; a consequential unresolved definition still belongs to the user. The
three new Task Coordination cases specify an incompatible aggregation, a
compatible asset handoff, and an unresolved material definition. They are not
executed model evaluations.

Other portable mechanisms are already represented: accepted-scope preservation,
verification applicability, source provenance and freshness, conditional
delegation, scoped recovery, and distinguishing installed instructions from live
activation and effectiveness. Engineering-specific test design, pooled-session
isolation, deployment, and process-runner implementation stay outside PM. No
Context Management policy or helper changes follow from this pass.

The empirical review does not establish that shorter guidance is better, that
popularity proves efficacy, or that either suite saves usage. Its actionable
maintenance question is what extra work an instruction requires. This pass found
no justification for another skill, blanket trimming, numerical quotas,
mandatory tooling, or a new measurement pipeline. The expected benefit of the
handoff check is earlier detection of an incompatible dependency; actual model
adherence and total-cost effects remain unmeasured.
