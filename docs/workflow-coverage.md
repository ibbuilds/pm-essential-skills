# PM workflow coverage — 26 October 2026

PM owns coordination, context, resource/source choice, proportionate execution,
workflow improvement and clear communication. Software design, product strategy,
coding, testing, deployment and operations remain SWE responsibilities. A compendium
should route to the right owner rather than make each suite duplicate the other.

The combined [70-entry source map](https://github.com/ibbuilds/swe-essential-skills/blob/main/docs/workflow-coverage.md)
inventories Matt Pocock, Ras Mic and Addy Osmani at pinned revisions and explicitly
separates existing/adapted methods, unbundled host adapters, exclusions and experiments.
It is a bounded mechanism comparison, not proof of every market skill or universal
effectiveness. Earlier [PM scope](pm-scope-coverage.md) and
[source research](research-and-edge-cases.md) retain their stated evidence limits.

Task Coordination now explicitly routes “grill me”/plan challenges and requested
stakeholder questionnaires to its local decision-interview reference. It retains
ready-question dependencies, user pacing, settled answers, bounded delegation and
concrete completion. It does not force an interview on ordinary clear work or
authorize external contact. Technical interviews belong to SWE Requirements.
