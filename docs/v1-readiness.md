# PM outcome contract and readiness

The product reduces avoidable total work and user intervention while preserving
the requested outcome, evidence and human control. The unit of value is a
completed task, including startup, reads, questions, handoffs, retries and checks.
Skill count, shorter text and a passing installer do not establish that value.

## The operating contract

| Work | Required useful behavior | When to do less |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Coordinate | Retain all accepted scope, prerequisites, owners and real completion | Clear small requests need no plan, tracker or delegation |
| Preserve context | Reuse decisions and location through related corrections; carry enough state for a justified authorized handoff | No repeated question just because another message arrived; all optional below-critical boundaries respect known transfer cost |
| Choose resources | Use suitable supported tools and capabilities for the work | No model switch, inventory, agent fanout or benchmark without a concrete need and authority |
| Retrieve evidence | Establish missing facts from relevant current sources, with coverage and provenance | Reuse sufficient findings; bound combined output without hiding failures or narrowing an exhaustive task |
| Execute | Match checks to affected claims, diagnose failures and finish active operations | Reuse applicable results; no unchanged retry-to-green or repeated broad checks |
| Improve workflow | Repair demonstrated friction at its owning instruction/helper | No new rulebook or skill for every one-off event |
| Communicate | Give the result, consequential choices and precise blockers in plain language | Keep full requested deliverables; omit repeated narration and artificial approval gates |

The seven skills are independent. Activate only useful owners and relevant
references; the host's existing abilities remain available. The source of truth
for each procedure is its skill folder, not this overview or a historical audit.

## Closure of the 26 October outcome review

The independent audit read all seven entrypoints and relevant helper/reference
contracts. It reproduced a negative-value context prompt: a new phase/outcome
bypassed the existing cost guard, and successive stays increased recommendation
strength without stronger evidence. Both mechanisms are corrected. Related
corrections now retain their logical outcome/location; explicit human prompting
preferences and required transfer authority remain binding.

`tests/test_context_value.py` freezes nine regression contracts. Five assertions
failed on the prior policy; all nine pass on the corrected one. Existing pending
choice, critical pressure, safe checkpoint, missing-context and authority cases
remain in the suite. Synthetic costs are supplied test data, not measured savings.
The independent baseline also repaired a real local report against a frozen
USD 49.00 expectation, preserving its title/note and exclusions. No user prompt,
chat mutation or remote source call was needed for that bounded task.

The completed candidate check ran all 329 repository tests (21 platform-specific
skips locally), validated seven skill packages and built all eight installer ZIPs.
A fresh independent decision simulation kept the heading correction and related
total row under the accepted outcome/location, and rejected an optional move
whose supplied total cost was 5 versus continuing cost 1. This is simulated
host decision evidence, not an actual chat transfer. Sixteen frozen helper
inputs also replayed successfully; unchanged inputs do not by themselves test
the model's semantic classification of a related correction.

Earlier [functional workflows](functional-validation-2026-10-25.md) and the
29 [replayable procedure observations](../evaluations/procedure-cases.json)
remain scoped evidence. The test suite and packaging checks verify mechanics;
they do not prove universal adherence or net subscription savings. Native
interactive rendering and real chat-transfer behavior were not newly exercised.

## Basis and adaptation

[OpenAI's skill guidance](https://developers.openai.com/blog/rethinking-skills-and-prompts-for-gpt-6-astra)
supports scoped routing and progressive disclosure;
[its eval method](https://developers.openai.com/blog/eval-skills) requires observed
outcomes and traces rather than appearance. [Anthropic's authoring guidance](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/agents-and-tools/agent-skills/best-practices)
ties specificity to fragility and asks whether instructions justify their cost.
[Matt Pocock's handoff](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/blob/5beb35b040e3de97e54fa9e386d7e42d15c295c2/skills/productivity/handoff/SKILL.md)
supports referencing durable artifacts instead of duplicating them. We retain
that mechanism with this project's host authorization, recovery and continuity
requirements; no third-party workflow or text is vendored.

Historical first-version assessments are retained in Git history. Their earlier
policy statements and test counts do not override the current skill sources.
Do not expand the suite merely to call it definitive: add a mechanism when an
actual failure or uncovered recurring task justifies it, then check its no-op case.
