# Decision interview review

The requested GrillMe comparison covers Task Coordination's deliberate plan
challenge, not a new planning phase for every task. The entrypoint already routes
explicit challenges to `references/decision-interviews.md`, including challenges
to accepted plans. Version 0.6.11 changes that conditional reference only; no
global agent rules, activation policy or other skills change.

## Mechanisms and correction

[Matt Pocock's grilling procedure](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/blob/main/skills/productivity/grilling/SKILL.md)
was checked at content blob `d2f09a18e6a6c65c55f1271ba08dfeaf06f0bed3`.
Its useful mechanisms are prerequisite-aware questions, actual investigation,
waiting for answers and recomputing after partial answers. In particular, running
research is an unresolved prerequisite. Existing attribution remains in the
reference. This is an original adaptation, not a verbatim imported template.

The previous closure wording allowed branches that were merely “investigated” or
“delegated” to count as finished. The revision requires usable evidence or an
explicit safe deferral. It distinguishes delegated research from a delivered
answer and bounded decision authority from an actual decision. Answers are checked
against constraints and prior answers; ambiguous assent does not choose among
alternatives. Closure checks the resulting plan against its outcome and a decisive
failure case. Deferred branches retain reason, owner and latest safe decision point.

The adaptation deliberately preserves neutral factual questions, supported
recommendations, user pacing, material scope, stop requests and existing execution
authority. It does not import mandatory subagents, agreement-biased questions,
exhaustion of every imaginable branch or a redundant final approval ritual.

## Observed application

A fresh independent executor received the revised skill and raw fictional venue,
budget and signup evidence, without this review or expected answers. Three turns
tested an accepted workshop plan with one question at a time:

- It identified 45 seated places versus 70 promised, rather than accepting the
  venue's unrelated standing capacity of 80.
- After the user proposed a second room, new evidence that none existed kept the
  capacity issue open. Delegated catering stayed pending within its $1,500 bound.
- When the user stopped questions, it retained 70 seated places, the $3,500 venue
  bound and the user's fallback authority. It reported unavailable venue/catering
  evidence and distinguished 40 registrations from a goal of 70. No booking occurred.

A separate accepted grammar-correction control returned the corrected sentence
directly, without an interview. These are synthetic application observations,
not native activation measurements or a comparative success rate against GrillMe.
Mechanical checks validate packaging and repository contracts, not universal
reasoning quality. No claim of perfection or measured superiority follows.
